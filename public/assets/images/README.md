# Custom Character & Sprites Directory 🐓

Place your custom character sprite and props here!

### 1. Character Sprite:
Replace `public/assets/character.png` (or `character.svg`) with your own image!
- **Recommended size:** 120x100 or square transparent PNG (e.g. 256x256, 512x512).
- **Format:** Transparent PNG or SVG.
- **Orientation:** Facing right.

### 2. Live Customizer In-Game:
You can also click the **"Customizer"** button in the game settings to upload and test any image or sound directly from your computer in real-time!
